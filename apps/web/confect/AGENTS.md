# Confect review context

## Code Review Rules

- Follow the real chain: `*.spec.ts` → `*.impl.ts` → called helpers/native interop → generated public wrapper. Effect 4 / Confect versions are pinned in `package.json`; read those installed declarations and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` before recommending APIs. `.middleware(RequireUser)` may be on the group or an individual function. Native `convexPublic*` interop is intentional and performs auth in its underlying handler. Neither form is automatically a bypass.
- Identity and ownership are persistence invariants. Check every supplied relationship and early return, and reads/deletion of poisoned historical rows. `uploads` purpose metadata never proves storage ownership. Internal functions rely on their internal visibility and validated trusted callers; do not require a fictitious end-user session on scheduled work. Changes to visibility deserve an explicit call-chain check.
- Respect transaction/runtime boundaries. Queries/mutations remain deterministic and use no external I/O; provider work is in Node actions. Mutations schedule durable follow-up work transactionally. Inspect retries, revision guards, interruption cleanup, and deletion races. Promise adapters at native Convex/HTTP boundaries are expected; do not wrap pure transforms in Effects for style.
- Evaluate growth, not just syntax: use indexes and bounded pages/context. Check fan-out, one-pagination-per-function constraints, large-account cursors, and response projections. UI payloads must omit vectors and unrelated private fields. Legacy compatibility paths are documented exceptions, not permission for new unbounded reads. Returns schemas are required by Effect FunctionSpecs; native interop may retain inferred validated contracts—avoid a blanket duplicate-validator finding.
- Generated adapters are evidence, not the repair target. Review schema/visibility/index changes in source and verify regeneration. For a suspected flaw, run the focused storage/planning/read-model suite or a seeded property probe. Report impact, concrete input, negative and positive controls, exact base/head, and an artifact or command. Never claim browser fixtures prove real Clerk/Gemini/Calendar delivery or Convex limits that the test harness does not model.
