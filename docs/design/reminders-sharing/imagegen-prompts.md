# Imagegen prompt

Built-in imagegen was used on 2026-09-22. The committed concept uses synthetic photos and illustrative system share targets. This is a review artifact, not proof of platform behavior.

Use case: ui-mockup. A high fidelity Wardrobe product UX review board in landscape with FOUR large, clearly legible phone-sized surfaces side by side. Overall title "A gentle reminder. A simple way to share." Subtitle "Concept C · proposed defaults · synthetic photos". Chalk #F7F3F5 page, dark aubergine #241426 text, mauve #D8C9DC and citron #DCE66E actions. Familiar native platform controls and system share sheets. No social network, public feed, followers, scores, badges, analytics jargon or implementation text. No private photos. Synthetic adult full-outfit photos in navy shirt, charcoal trousers, white shoes where needed. Screens large enough to read, generous spacing.

SCREEN 1 header above "No plan today · Android". A realistic Android lock screen with time "12:00" and a Wardrobe push notification. EXACT notification title "A moment for your fit?" and body "Capture what you're wearing today." Native action "Fit check". No event names, garment details or photos inside notification. Under phone annotation outside UI "Once at midday, only if no plan or wear is recorded."

SCREEN 2 header "Reminder settings · iOS". Native iOS navigation back chevron, title "Reminders". Two native grouped switch rows: "Planned outfits" enabled and short subtext "At the event start"; "Days without a plan" enabled and subtext "Once at midday". Row "Midday" value "12:00 PM". Row "Quiet hours" value "9 PM – 9 AM". Row "Daily limit" value "2 reminders". Small secondary line "At least 3 hours apart". Row "Deliver to" value "This phone". Low-emphasis native helper text "Reminders stop when you record your outfit." Footer "You can change these anytime." These are proposed defaults, not approval or a working settings screen. Exact rows, no extra toggle clutter.

SCREEN 3 header "Share fit · iOS". Realistic iOS fit photo detail dimmed behind a native iOS share sheet. The sheet shows a tiny preview of the synthetic mirror outfit photo and text "Fit photo" "Image". Below show familiar native app targets e.g AirDrop, Messages, Mail in an OS-owned app row. Generic contact initials may be visible in native system contact suggestion row but NO Wardrobe contacts list. Lower native sheet actions "Copy" and "Save Image". This is handing image pixels to native OS sharing, not sharing a website link. Do not show a URL anywhere. No caption builder or post button. No "sent" toast.

SCREEN 4 header "Image export · mobile web". Wardrobe Fit detail with synthetic full-length outfit photo in background, a restrained bottom panel labeled "Share fit". Image preview with brief plain text "Share this photo". Three actions: "Share image" primary, "Copy image", "Download image". Small helper "Available options depend on your browser." No destination choice inside Wardrobe, no public link, no social controls. Share button invokes the operating system when supported. Labels readable.
Below entire board a thin divider and small text "Your phone handles the destination. Wardrobe shares only the image." Keep platform iconography credible; do not imply every device has identical share targets.
